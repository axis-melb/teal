Building permits in Victoria

A building permit confirms your project meets the Building Act 1993, the Building Regulations 2018 and the National Construction Code (NCC). We act as Relevant Building Surveyor (RBS) across all building classes.

What we issue permits for

  • New buildsClass 1-10 authorisations.
  • RenovationsExtensions and alterations.
  • Change of useReclassification of an existing building.
  • ComplianceWork assessed against the Building Act 1993 and the Building Professional Code of Conduct.
