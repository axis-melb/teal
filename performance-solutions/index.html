Performance Solutions under the NCC

A Performance Solution is an alternative route to meeting the National Construction Code (NCC). It may depart from the Deemed-to-Satisfy (DTS) provisions, but must still meet the relevant Performance Requirements, and that compliance has to be clearly demonstrated.

For commercial and multi-residential work we coordinate with fire engineers, architects and independent surveyors so the regulatory approach stays consistent across disciplines.
