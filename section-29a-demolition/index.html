Section 29A demolition consent

Section 29A demolition consent is generally required where works change 50% or more of a building's volume, or alter its facade. We coordinate the consent process with council ahead of your building permit.

Works commonly affected

  • External walls
  • Balconies
  • Roofs and chimneys
  • Heritage elements
